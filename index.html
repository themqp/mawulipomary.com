The Man Behind the Hats

A Story of Purpose,
Passion & Polymathy

Based: Everywhere

"I am not defined by a single title. I am the sum of every experience, every country visited, every problem solved, and every conversation sparked."

Mawuli Pomary, known simply as MQP, is a Ghanaian engineer, scholar, and thinker who has never been content to exist within the boundaries of a single discipline. Born with an insatiable curiosity and a gift for seeing systems where others see chaos, MQP has built a life at the crossroads of engineering precision, data-driven insight, and human-centered storytelling.

A proud Erasmus Mundus Scholar, MQP brings a European academic pedigree to African ingenuity, a powerful combination that has shaped a truly global perspective. Whether he is analyzing complex datasets, standing before an audience, launching a venture, or charting sustainability pathways, MQP operates with the conviction that versatility is not a weakness. It is a superpower.

03
The Winding Road

A Journey of Becoming

Ghana · The Beginning
Foundation & First Principles
Ghana · The Roots

Every great story begins with a foundation. Raised with a deep appreciation for education, community, and excellence, MQP's Ghanaian upbringing instilled values of resilience, resourcefulness, and responsibility. The engineering mindset was born here, solving everyday problems with whatever tools were available.

Origin Story
Russia · Engineering Studies
Bachelor of Engineering
Russia

Four years of rigorous engineering education in Russia shaped not just technical competence but a fundamentally different way of approaching problems. Coursework spanning thermodynamics, systems design, mathematics, and applied sciences, combined with practical project work, built the analytical framework MQP operates from today. Studying in Russia also delivered a formative layer of cross-cultural immersion that would become a defining thread through his career.

Technical Formation
Erasmus Mundus · The Selection
Prestigious European Scholarship Award
European Commission Scholarship Programme

A defining moment. Being selected as an Erasmus Mundus Scholar is among the most competitive academic honours available globally, awarded to exceptional students from developing nations. This recognition opened doors to European universities, international networks, and a perspective-shifting academic journey across multiple countries.

Career Milestone
Europe · Graduate Studies
Erasmus Mundus Programme
Multiple European Universities

Studying across European institutions exposed MQP to diverse methodological traditions, research cultures, and intellectual communities. Courses spanning sustainability, data science, engineering management, and systems thinking formed a rare interdisciplinary blend. Collaborating with peers from 40+ countries accelerated both technical skills and global perspective in equal measure.

Academic Peak
The Data Analytics Era
Data Analyst & Strategy Consultant
USA & Various Organisations

Engineering skills met the world of data. MQP pivoted into analytics work, designing dashboards, building models, and deriving actionable insights from complex datasets. The combination of engineering rigour and data literacy made him a uniquely valuable voice in cross-functional teams navigating data-driven transformation.

Skills Expansion
The Speaker Emerges
Conference Speaker & Thought Leader
Conferences, Universities & Community Platforms

The realisation that ideas need voices. MQP stepped onto stages, local, regional, and international, sharing perspectives on engineering, sustainability, data, and the African opportunity. His authentic, story-driven speaking style resonates across cultural lines and professional contexts alike.

Voice Amplified
Present · The Nomadic Builder
Entrepreneur, Consultant & Global Nomad
Wherever the Work Takes Him

Today, MQP operates at the intersection of all his identities. Building ventures, consulting organisations on sustainability and data strategy, mentoring emerging talent, and continuing to roam the world, collecting experiences, connections, and stories. The story of MQP is still being written.

Moves Through the World

MQP's Operating Principle
01
Curiosity Before Convention

The world rewards specialists, but the future belongs to those who can connect dots across disciplines. MQP chooses curiosity, learning something new, entering unfamiliar rooms, and asking the questions no specialist would think to ask.

02
Action as the Antidote to Paralysis

Perfect information never arrives. MQP builds, launches, speaks, and ships, learning through doing, iterating in public, and treating failure as data. Motion beats meditation when the world needs solutions now.

03
Roots That Enable Wings

A global citizen first and foremost, but one who never forgets where he came from. African values of community, Ubuntu, and long-term thinking infuse every international endeavour. The world is richer for the diversity MQP carries into every room.

04
Building for Beyond

Every decision is filtered through a simple question: does this build something worth leaving behind? Sustainability is not a trend. It is the ethical obligation of anyone with the tools and platform to make a lasting difference.
